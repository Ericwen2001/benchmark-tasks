# Releasable camera mount

The rear shoe enters a keyed guide through a four-face tapered nose. Its leading surface retracts the inclined spring tooth during insertion. At the original seated camera pose, the tooth returns into the side notch. The guide and stop constrain the housing; the tooth prevents withdrawal.

An accessible external pull tab is part of the same rigid sliding body as the tooth. Pulling the tab outward along the guide retracts the tooth and permits withdrawal. The tab is not a software trigger. The model uses a prismatic guide with a 150 N/m spring, 0.15 N s/m damping and 5 mm travel. All shoe, guide, tooth and tab surfaces have collision geometry. There is no fixed joint connecting camera and wrist.

The task is to reinstall the camera at its original mounting position and orientation. Pulling the tab permits removal when resetting the task.
